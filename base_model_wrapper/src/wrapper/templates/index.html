What you get

An endpoint built for research on the model itself, not just its outputs.

True base models

Raw /v1/completions — no chat template, no RLHF, no system prompt. The model as it came out of pretraining.

Token-level access

Top-k logprobs on generated tokens and prompt_logprobs on the input — for likelihood and interpretability work. Plus echo and SSE streaming.

OpenAI compatible

Point any OpenAI client at one base URL. Full sampling controls — temperature, top_p, top_k, min_p, penalties, and seed.

Browser workbench

Prompt the models straight from the browser to get a feel for them before you (ask your friendly LLMs to) write a line of code.
